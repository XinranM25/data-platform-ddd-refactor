# Domain packs

A domain pack lists the concepts, traps and typical bounded contexts of one industry's data platforms. It sharpens the glossary and context-map steps; it never replaces the user's own decisions.

| Pack | Covers |
|---|---|
| [energy.md](energy.md) | European electricity markets and power trading: renewable assets, market prices, metering, forecasts, trading, settlement |

## Using a pack

- Read it after `references/lessons.md`, before drafting the glossary.
- Turn its traps into questions, checked against the user's data first.
- Its typical contexts are a starting point for Step 2, not a target.

## Writing a new pack

Keep the same sections: core concepts, time and units, traps, typical contexts, typical sources, glossary questions to ask. Write only what holds across companies in that industry, and only what you can check; mark anything that varies by country or market. Packs for finance and trading, IoT and industrial telemetry, logistics and retail analytics would fit next.
